Inferno is a high-octane speedrunning game set in the fiery depths of Hell, drawing inspiration from titles like Ghostrunner and Neon White. You play as a relentless warrior, equipped with supernatural abilities that allow you to dash, jump, and strike with lightning-fast precision through twisted infernal landscapes.

Your goal is simple: reach the end of each level as fast as possible. But in Hell, nothing comes easy. Demonic creatures stand in your way, and to survive alone, you’ll need to slay every monster that dares to challenge you. Each kill grants you a surge of demonic energy, which you can harvest to enhance your abilities, making you faster, stronger, and more agile with every victory.
<br><br>
Inferno encourages exploration and mastery. Every level is packed with hidden paths, alternate routes, and clever shortcuts that reward players who take risks and think creatively. Whether you're shaving milliseconds off your time or discovering new strategies, every run brings you closer to the top of the global leaderboard.
<br><br>
Face the flames, conquer the damned, and become the fastest soul to ever blaze through Hell.

I was responsible for developing the entire character controller from the ground up. This included implementing a variety of core movement and combat mechanics such as dashing, double jumping, and shooting, as well as creating a responsive and intuitive camera system. I also designed and integrated a wall-running system, allowing for fluid traversal across vertical surfaces and adding depth to the game's fast-paced movement. The controller was built with a focus on responsiveness, player feedback, and momentum, ensuring that every action feels smooth and satisfying—crucial for a speedrunning game like Inferno.
