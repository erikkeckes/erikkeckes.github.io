Pac-Man is a timeless classic, but what if you could play it in 3D and with a friend? Enter Soul Maze, a modern reimagining of the arcade legend with exciting new mechanics and cooperative gameplay.
<br>
In Soul Maze, two players take on the roles of Pac-Men, working together to collect all the points scattered across the maze. To maintain the original game's tactical elements, players must communicate and coordinate their movements. A unique leaning mechanic allows players to peek around corners, revealing more of the maze to plan their next move.
<br>
But collecting points won’t be easy—four relentless ghosts are always on the hunt. To counter this threat, each player has a distinct role:

* The Hunter can eliminate ghosts.

* The Collector focuses on gathering points.
<br>
However, strategy is key! If a ghost is closing in, players can switch roles on the fly, turning the tide of the chase. Want to take it a step further? You can use yourself as bait, luring ghosts into a trap before striking them down.
<br>
But don’t get too comfortable—ghosts never stay gone for long. They will always return, keeping the challenge alive and forcing you to stay on your toes. Master teamwork, outmaneuver the ghosts, and claim victory in Soul Maze!
