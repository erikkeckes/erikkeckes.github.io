Citizen Conflict is a hero shooter that delivers fast-paced arena action and large-scale Battle Royale battles. Join the relentless struggle for dominance among three rival factions, each with unique heroes. Set in a futuristic world, the game features high-tech maps, advanced weaponry, and unconventional gear to enhance the combat experience.

<h3>Game Mechanics</h3>
I was responsible for developing, maintaining, and refining various abilities and mechanics to ensure seamless integration into the gameplay experience. 
* Tactical Sprint a faster version of the standard sprint that requires stamina to activate. This ability included custom effects. (network synchronization, visuas)
<h3>Optimization</h3>
My role also involved optimizing the game's performance, focusing on networking, CPU, and GPU efficiency. I extensively used Unreal Insights and other profiling tools to identify and resolve bottlenecks, with a primary emphasis on networking and CPU optimization. This required creative solutions to enhance overall performance.

* CPU Optimization: Improved gameplay mechanics, reduced load times, and eliminated potential bottlenecks affecting performance.

* Networking Optimization: Minimized network calls by leveraging already synchronized properties, reduced overall data footprint, and optimized network efficiency to improve player latency and overall experience.
<h3>Prototyping</h3> 
I collaborated with game designers on prototyping, ensuring rapid development and quick iterations of potential systems. Fast iteration was crucial for refining ideas and assessing their feasibility within the game.
<h3>Bug Fixes:</h3> 
As part of the development team, resolving bugs was crucial to ensuring the stability and polish of existing systems. This included addressing performance issues, eliminating unintended behavior, and improving overall quality.

* Internal Framework: Maintained and improved an internal framework reused across multiple games, enabling faster prototyping of new ideas.
